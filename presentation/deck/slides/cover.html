Seven departments, one link. Someone has to slow down.

Good morning. Our project answers one question: when a company network is full, who should be slowed down, and can the system explain why? This time we did not simulate the network. Phones and laptops join department slices over a Wi-Fi hotspot, and every byte they download goes through a real shaped link. The bars on the right are departments sharing that link. The canteen bar is shorter: by the end you will see why the canteen, and not the clinic, gives way, and you will see it on measured traffic.
