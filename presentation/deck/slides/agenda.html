Here is the plan. First some background: what network slicing is and how an enterprise uses it. Then the problem statement: what happens when slices compete for a full link, and why the usual label-based approach makes bad decisions. Then our solution, the control loop and the CBHA algorithm. Then the testbed we built: a gateway that every byte passes through, a controller, and phones on a hotspot. Then the measured results with arbitration off and on. Finally the live demo, the honest limits and what comes next.
