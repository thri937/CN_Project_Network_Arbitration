What’s new

Four ideas, working together

1

Business value, not labels

It cannot even see the URLLC or eMBB tags.

2

Small cuts, not one big cut

Spreads the pain thinly and fairly.

3

Safety is a rule, not a score

Nothing can outvote it.

4

Proved on real traffic

Real phones, a real shaped link, every 2 s recorded.

Each idea exists somewhere. Combining all four is new.

Here is what is new. One: it decides by business value, and structurally cannot read network labels; the input has no field for them. Two: a convex harm curve, shaped by each department’s recovery tolerance, spreads small cuts instead of one deep cut, which a priority sort cannot do. Three: life-safety is a hard constraint, removed before optimisation, not a large weight. Four: we did not stop at a simulation. The decisions drive a real shaped link that real devices use, and every two-second window is recorded, so the effect of each decision is measured, not assumed.
