Let’s be honest about where it stands. What is real: bytes really flow through our gateway, the limits are really enforced, the delay and loss are really measured, phones really join over the laptop’s hotspot, and the decisions, rules and explanations are the full working system. What is scaled down: the link is 1 megabit per second, not 10 gigabits, so seven phones can fill it; the shaping is done in our own software, not in a 5G radio or a kernel; and the measured results so far come from software agents on one laptop, over loopback. The phone run is supported and ready; it is the next measurement.
