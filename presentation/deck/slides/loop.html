Fully automatic. Step 4 is where the thinking happens.

The whole system is one loop, repeated every two seconds. Measure: the gateway reports each slice’s demand, carried traffic, drops and probe latency from the last two seconds of real traffic. Detect a shortage, calmly: demand must be above 102 percent of the link on two readings in a row, and it needs three calm readings to call it over. Score each slice by business value. Decide how much to take from each, the clever part. Apply: push the new speed limits to the gateway, which enforces them on the next 20-millisecond tick. Restore them when the department’s recovery time is up, from 30 seconds to 30 minutes. And record a permanent explanation.
