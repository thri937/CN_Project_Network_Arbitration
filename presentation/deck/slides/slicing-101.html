Network slicing is one of the core ideas of 5G. Instead of one network that treats all traffic the same, the operator carves the physical network into several logical, end-to-end networks called slices. Each slice gets its own guarantees — bandwidth, latency, reliability — written down as a service-level agreement. 3GPP identifies a slice with an S-NSSAI: the Slice/Service Type, or SST, plus an optional Slice Differentiator, SD. The standard types are eMBB for high bandwidth, URLLC for ultra-reliable low latency, mMTC for massive IoT, and V2X for vehicles. Our project models slices natively with S-NSSAI, so connecting to a real 5G core later needs no data migration.
