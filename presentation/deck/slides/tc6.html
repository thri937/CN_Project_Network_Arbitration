Cut slices still wait ~0.5 s, but in their own queue. CCTV and Office (cut) look the same: 626→479 and 573→476 ms.

Here is every slice from the same run, grey with arbitration off and blue or orange with it on. Off, everyone sits near half a second, and the canteen near a full second. On, the protected slices, clinic, factory and lab, drop to about 22 milliseconds. The slices that were cut, shown in orange, still wait about half a second. That is not the shared queue any more: each now waits in its own slice queue, which holds half a second at its new, lower limit and stays full because it asks for more than its limit. That is exactly where we want the waiting to happen: in the slices the business chose to slow, not in the clinic.
