Each slice comes with a contract. Bandwidth has three levels: a guaranteed floor the system may never cut below, a normal share it is restored to after any cut, and a burst ceiling. For the clinic: a 40 kbps floor, 60 normal, 90 burst. Then there are quality targets: latency at most 150 milliseconds at the 95th percentile, jitter at most 25, packet loss at most 0.1 percent, at least 40 kbps, and five-nines availability. In this project these are not modelled numbers: the gateway measures each of them every two seconds from the slice’s own traffic and probes, and the dashboard compares them with the targets.
