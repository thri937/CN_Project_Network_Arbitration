This is the live demo. One: each department’s phone scans its QR code from the dashboard and appears in the devices list. Two: press Run on TC3, the lunch-hour rush, and switch automatic arbitration off. Within a minute the clinic phone’s latency climbs to about half a second and it starts losing data, although its own limit has not changed: everyone is stuck in the same full queue. Three: switch arbitration on. Within a few seconds the engine cuts the canteen and the other cheap slices; after about 25 seconds the shared queue has drained and the clinic is back to tens of milliseconds with no loss. Four: open the newest decision and read the explanation and the alternatives it rejected; the canteen phone shows its own explanation too. Finally run TC5 to return to normal, and download the CSV recording of the whole demo.
