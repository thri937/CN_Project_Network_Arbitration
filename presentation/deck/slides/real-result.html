This is a real decision from our test run of the lunch-hour rush: the canteen asks for double its share, office and cameras run flat out, the warehouse is busy. Demand reached 1,316 kbps on a 950 kbps link. Under the current limits the slices could carry 1,173 kbps, so 251.5 kbps had to be freed, including the 3 percent safety margin. The canteen gave up 80 percent, down to its 28 kbps minimum. Office Wi-Fi was cut to 217, the warehouse to 121, the cameras to 211. The clinic was never cut, and the factory and quality lab were untouched. Notice: the canteen’s premium URLLC label did not protect it, and the lab’s standard eMBB label did not expose it. The gateway was enforcing the new limits four seconds after the load changed.
