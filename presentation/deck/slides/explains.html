If a department head asks why their system was slowed at lunch, the answer is a page in the app, not a guess. The text here follows the engine’s own summary template, with the numbers from our lunch-rush run rounded. Each decision records what was happening, who was cut and by how much, why in plain sentences with real numbers, the alternative plans it priced and rejected, and the rules in force at that moment. And in this testbed the department sees it too: the phone of a slice that was cut shows the explanation on its own page. The record is append-only: there is no edit or delete button anywhere, even for administrators.
