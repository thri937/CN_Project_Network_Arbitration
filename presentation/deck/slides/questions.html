The QoS label (5QI, slice type) is not an input — at all.

Our system ignores network labels entirely and asks six business questions about each department. How critical is it — the clinic is mission critical, the canteen best effort. What does an outage cost per minute — the factory line about 8,400 pounds, the canteen screens 15. Are there safety or legal obligations — the clinic is life-safety, the quality lab regulated. Do customers notice? How long can it cope with being slowed — its recovery tolerance. And was it slowed down recently, so the burden can rotate. The QoS class literally isn’t a field in the engine’s input, so it cannot influence the outcome.
