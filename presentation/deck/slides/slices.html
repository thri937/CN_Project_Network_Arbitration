In an enterprise, departments own the slices, and each department matters differently to the business.

In a company network, slicing gives each department’s systems its own reserved lane. Our testbed has seven: the clinic’s patient telemetry, factory automation, the quality lab’s instruments, warehouse operations, security cameras, office Wi-Fi and the canteen menu screens. Next to each lane is its 5G slice type. Notice two things. The canteen screens were set up as URLLC, the same premium type as the clinic, simply because of the template someone chose. And the regulated quality lab was set up as plain eMBB. Remember both; they are the root of the problem.
