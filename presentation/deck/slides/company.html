7 departments · 7 slices · 1,480 kbps promised on a 1 Mbps link

Our testbed company has seven departments, each with one slice: the clinic, the factory, the quality lab, the warehouse, security, office IT and the canteen. Each slice is promised a normal share and a guaranteed minimum, and each has a six-character join code; a phone types the code, or scans a QR code from the dashboard, to join that department’s slice. Why kilobits and a 1 Mbps link? So that seven phones on one laptop hotspot can really overload the link without the Wi-Fi itself becoming the bottleneck. The total promised is 1,480 kbps.
