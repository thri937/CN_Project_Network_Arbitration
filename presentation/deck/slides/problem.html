Choosing who is network arbitration.

The testbed link is 1 Mbps, of which 950 kbps is offered to slices; 50 is kept back. But the slices were promised 1,480 kbps in total, 156 percent. That is normal, like a gym selling more memberships than it has machines, because not everyone comes at once. The problem appears when real demand goes above 950 kbps. Then the link’s queue fills up, and something must be slowed down. Deciding who, and by how much, is network arbitration.
