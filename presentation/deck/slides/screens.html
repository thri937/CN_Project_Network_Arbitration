There are two web apps. The operator dashboard is the control room. Its home page, Slices and devices, shows every slice with its measured demand, carried traffic, latency and loss, a QR code for each department, the scenario buttons, the switch for automatic arbitration, the list of joined devices and the recording download. Contention and Decisions show when shortages happened and the full reasoning for each decision, with an override for administrators. Live charts plot latency and traffic. Slice contracts, Departments, Business policy and the Audit log hold the rules. The second app is the department page on each phone: it joins with a code, chooses how much to download, and shows its slice’s limit, demand, latency and loss every second, plus the controller’s explanation when its slice is cut.
