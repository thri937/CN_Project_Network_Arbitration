Three rules it can never break

The clinic is never touched

Removed before the maths even starts.

Never below the promised minimum

Canteen never below 28 kbps.

A capped cut per department

Canteen up to 80%. Factory only 15%.

A score can be outvoted. A rule cannot.

Three hard limits. First, life-safety slices like the clinic are taken out of the calculation before it begins, so no combination of numbers can ever cut them. Many systems instead give safety a large weight, but a weight can be outvoted when enough other factors line up; a constraint cannot. Second, every slice has a contractual minimum it never goes below; the canteen screens, for example, never drop below 28 kbps. Third, each department has a maximum cut: 80 percent for the canteen, 20 for the quality lab, 15 for the factory. When those rules leave too little to free, the engine does not break them: it reports a partial plan and alerts the operator.
