Cost on a log scale

Versioned policy

Admins change weights; old decisions keep theirs.

The business score is a weighted sum of seven factors, each first normalised to a 0 to 100 scale. In the default policy, department criticality counts 26 percent, downtime cost 20, business importance 16, compliance 14, customer impact 12, operational importance 7 and recovery intolerance 5; the weights must total exactly 100 percent. Downtime cost is log-normalised: the reference cost maps to 50 and each tenfold increase adds only about 25 points, so a department cannot win simply by declaring an enormous number. The policy is versioned and immutable once active — editing creates a new draft — and every decision stores a snapshot of the policy it used, so it stays explainable after the rules change.
